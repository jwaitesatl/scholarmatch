# ScholarMatch - Complete Business Plan
## Version 1.0 | March 21, 2026

---

# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**Business Name:** ScholarMatch  
**Domain:** scholarmatch.io  
**Email:** @scholarmatch.io  
**Mission:** Connect students with scholarships they actually qualify for — automatically.  
**Target:** High school juniors/seniors (16-22) + parents + adult learners  
**Revenue Model:** Freemium SaaS ($0-$49.99/month)

---

# PRODUCT OFFERING

## Tiers

| Tier | Price | Features |
|------|-------|----------|
| **Free** | $0 | 5 matches/month, email delivery, basic filtering |
| **Premium** | $9.99/mo | Unlimited matches, SMS reminders, essay tips, deadline alerts |
| **Complete** | $49.99 | Everything in Premium + 1-on-1 application review, essay editing, interview prep |

## Key Features (Built)
- Student signup form with 24+ background categories
- GPA up to 5.0 (weighted)
- State of residence (for local scholarships)
- Phone number (for SMS reminders)
- Live matching algorithm
- 30+ scholarships in database
- Responsive design (mobile-first)
