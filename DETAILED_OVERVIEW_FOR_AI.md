## THE CONCEPT

**What is ScholarMatch?**
A personalized scholarship matching platform that connects students with scholarships they actually qualify for based on their unique background, GPA, major, location, and life circumstances.

**The Problem:**
- $6B+ in scholarships go unclaimed every year
- Students spend hours searching for scholarships
- Existing platforms return generic results that students don't qualify for
- No platform matches on personal background factors (first-gen, foster care, cancer survivor, military family, etc.)

**The Solution:**
AI-powered matching that considers 24+ personal factors to find scholarships the student actually qualifies for, delivered daily to their inbox.

## WHAT WE'VE BUILT (MVP)

### Live Website
- **URL:** scholarmatch.io
- **Hosted on:** Netlify
- **Email:** @scholarmatch.io (Microsoft 365)

### Product Features (Completed)
1. **Student Signup Form** with:
   - Name, email, phone (for SMS)
   - State of residence (for local scholarships)
   - GPA (up to 5.0 for weighted)
   - Grade level (8th grade through graduate, adult learner)
   - Major/career goal
   - Citizenship status (US, PR, DACA, international, refugee)

2. **24 Background Categories:**
   - First-generation college student
   - Low-income / Pell Grant eligible
   - Veteran / Active Duty Military
   - Military family (parent/spouse deployed)
   - Child of law enforcement
   - Child of firefighter/EMT
   - Cancer survivor (self or family)
   - Chronic illness / disability
   - Foster care / aged out
   - Homeless / at risk
   - Single parent student
   - LGBTQ+ student
   - Minority / underrepresented
   - Rural / small town student
   - Adopted / ward of court
   - Religious affiliation
   - Rotary / Lions / Kiwanis member
   - JCI member
   - 4-H member
   - FBLA / DECA / SkillsUSA member
   - Athlete
   - Arts / music / creative
   - Community service
   - Work-study / part-time job

3. **Live Matching Algorithm** - scores and ranks scholarships based on:
   - GPA match
   - Major relevance
   - Background category match (bonus points)
   - Deadline proximity

4. **30+ Sample Scholarships** in database with:
   - Award amounts ($1,000 - $40,000)
   - Deadlines
   - Requirements (GPA, citizenship, year)
   - Categories they match to

5. **Responsive Design** - works on mobile, tablet, desktop
